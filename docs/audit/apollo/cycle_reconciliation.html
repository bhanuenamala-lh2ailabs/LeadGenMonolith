Ask Apollo for

  • Credit usage history by team member and by surface (Settings, Billing, Credit usage and AI, History) for each Pacific day above, starting with 29 and 30 Sep, 21 and 22 Sep.
  • The account timezone, to confirm the day boundary.
  • API keys: created date and last used for every key. The key was swapped on 29 Sep at 12:51; six keys have existed since 18 Aug. Delete the old ones.
  • Whether Apollo keeps access logs (IP addresses) for your key. Its public docs do not say.

What the evidence leans towards

  • Untraced spend arrives in bursts and is quiet overnight (3 credits in 11 h 43 min on 27 to 28 Sep), which fits people working the same hours.
  • The key never appeared in git, archives or mail, and no workflow or scheduled job calls Apollo. A leak by an outsider is the least supported explanation.
  • Most likely: teammates in the Apollo web app on the shared login, or scripts run elsewhere with a copy of the key. Unproven until Apollo's per-member history says so.
  • No script in the three repos explains the 22 Sep Waterfall and Email credits.
